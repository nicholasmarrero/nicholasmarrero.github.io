--- title: "Camera Tracking System" excerpt: "This project is a fast moving camera tracking system that can follow objects smoothly. The system collects data from either ROS2 publisher nodes or an external module. Then based on the location of system, the motors in the gimbal direct the camera to follow the object. Through the design project I gained expierence using ROS2, Fusion360, and soldering. Design Goals Smooth tracking so footage can idetify if landing gear excutes Adjust for latency so object stays centered 20 minutes of active use and 1 hour idle Pan rate of 180°/sec
