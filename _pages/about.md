Hello, I am Nicholas Marrero. I am a freshman at Virginia Tech studying Electrical Engineering. Outside of academics I am involved in two design teams and one service organizion.

Virginia Tech's BOLT develops a custom electric superbike. The bike runs at 600V and a top speed of 160mph. I am currently working on a battery pack. The goal is to keep laptops powered while out on the field, so the bikes data can be monitored. This project involes CAD with SOLIDWORKS and PCB design using ALTIUM.

MachWorks at Virginia Tech is working to be the first undergraduate team to build a supersonic aircraft using only air-breathing engines. My current project is designing a camera tracking system that monitors the aircraft for the full duration of the flight.

Service Without Borders at VT is an organization driven by our motto, Ut Prosim. We am to give back to the local communitty and extend our reach outside the United States to other communities in need. I hope to aid in the Nepal Teams mission after the school year ends.
